SMART-CONTRACT WALLETS (SAFE, MULTISIG)

  

    TPRO held in a smart-contract wallet is a special case: that address is a contract deployed on Ethereum
    (or Polygon), and the same address on Base belongs to nobody. So such rows are marked
    "contract wallet - designation needed" (this page shows the marker when you look the address up).
    The owners designate a Base address they control by sending one transaction from the contract wallet on
    Ethereum to the published designation address , with the Base address in
    the transaction data - only the owners can send it, and anyone can check it on Etherscan. Designations made before
    the final table are in the table; later ones are served from a public reserve during the whole claim window. Ask in
    the official community channel - and never "migrate" by sending old tokens anywhere:
    nothing in this process asks you to send tokens.
  


  
FAQ

  

    
My address is not in the table. Why?

    
The table is a snapshot of the old token at two pinned blocks (Ethereum and Polygon). An address is missing
    if it held no TPRO at those blocks: tokens bought or received later, tokens held on an exchange (withdraw to a
    wallet you control before the snapshot), or tokens in a contract address (see the notice above - the page marks
    those).

    
I had TPRO staked, in a vesting contract, or on Polygon.

    
Staking deposits and unclaimed vesting balances are credited to the address that owns them, and Polygon
    balances are credited to the same address natively. Everything an address held is merged into one row, claimed
    once, on Base.

    
Why the whole row in one transaction?

    
The contract pays each row exactly once, in full, at 1:1. Nothing to split, nothing to time, no penalty. The
    amount is fixed by the published table and cannot be changed by anyone, including us.

    
Can somebody else claim my row?

    
No. The contract pays a row only to the address itself: the transaction must be sent by that address.

    
What does it cost?

    
The Base network fee for one transaction, paid from your own wallet. Nobody sponsors it and nobody is entitled
    to ask you for anything.

    
What happens after the closing date?

    
The claim window is 36 months from launch (the exact moment is in STATUS). After it, claims stop and the
    unclaimed remainder is burned. A claim must be mined before that moment - claim early.

    
Who holds the keys to the claim contract?

    
Nobody. The claim contract has no owner, no pause, no upgrade and no rescue function; its table root is
    immutable. The token has no owner, no mint, no pause and no fees; its supply can only go down.

    
Can I verify this independently?

    
Yes. The snapshot tool and the published table are open, and the table root pinned on this page is the same
    root the claim contract stores. The verification guide is published together with the tool.
